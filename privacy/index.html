roaml Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Tyler Luedtke ("we," "us," or "our") operates the roaml mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use roaml, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using roaml, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

CategoryDataPurpose
Account Email address, display name, username, optional bio, optional avatar photo Authentication and your public profile
Location Approximate device location (collected only while the app is in the foreground) Verifying you are near a venue when you check in, and showing distance to nearby venues on the map
Check-in history Venue name, category, timestamp, optional star rating, optional note, optional vibe tags Core social feature — displayed in your feed and profile
Want-to-go list Saved venue names Your personal wishlist — kept private and never shared with other users

What we do not collect: roaml never stores your precise GPS coordinates. Location is used transiently, on your device, only to verify proximity to a venue at the moment of check-in — only the resolved venue name and category are saved to our database.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and public profile
  • To operate core app features: check-ins, feed, want-to-go list, follows, and reactions
  • To verify proximity to a venue when you check in and to show distances on the map
  • To send push notifications related to app activity (e.g., new followers, reactions)
  • To manage and validate premium subscription access
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the App

Data Retention

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account remains active, so that we can provide the App's core features. If you delete your account, your data is permanently and immediately removed as described below — we do not keep backups of deleted account data beyond what is required for short-term database backup rotation, which is automatically purged on a routine schedule.

Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the App:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account (at the bottom of the Settings sheet, below Sign Out)

When you delete your account, we permanently remove: your check-ins, want-to-go list, follows and follow requests, reactions, notifications and notification preferences, push token, taste profile, and profile information. Your avatar is deleted from storage and your authentication record is deleted; database constraints automatically remove all associated rows.

You may also contact us at hello@roaml.app to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.

Children's Privacy

roaml is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@roaml.app and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@roaml.app.
